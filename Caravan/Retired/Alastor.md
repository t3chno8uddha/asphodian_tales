An Oracle from [[Ensiferia]], who left his clan to roam the world and battle strong opponents. Spiritually limited, but compensates with brute force.

Punished in good faith by the god [[Lutrios]] in 1413, giving him intense spiritual power over which he has no control.

In 1441, the last remnant of Lutrios' soul resides as a sapling inside his soul.
