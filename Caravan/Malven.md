Mavros suffered the consequences of an Execration he cast - unable to remember what it made happen, and afflicting himself with a grave illness. Seeking help for it, and being rejected by Guru [[Dizang]], he learned the heretical ways and sealed himself inside of a mask.
The mask was thereby found by sailors, and it was transported from Ehelden to [[Infernait]] to be burnt, when the ship crashed into the [[Phantom Saltwaters]].
