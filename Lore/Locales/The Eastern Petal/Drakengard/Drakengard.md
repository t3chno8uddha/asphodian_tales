The mountain range where **Draca** built her own home. Worships Draca. 

Theological and political tension between Drakengard and **[[Wonderlay]]** drove **Cronika** to attack her sister Draca in **1400**, dying by accident in the attempt and scattering her spirit into her godblood descendants.

The mountain range is in fact a colossal dragon - Draca's Empyrean. Its head is overlooking the World Chasm and its tail is the rocks on which Lanaganne is built on. The mountains quakes reflect changes in Draca's mood.

Drakengard is the target of **Rafael's** vengeance, and in **1441** the launch-point of Draca's world-ending war once she learns her son **Daedalus** conspired against her.
