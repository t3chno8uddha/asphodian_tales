A turbulent valley, formed by the whims of a trickster deity, with the sole reason of making travel to and from the [[Heathen Homestead]] infinitely more difficult than ever need be.

The paths of the canyon change with the rising of the sun, rendering the very endeavour of mapping the canyon a fool's errand.
