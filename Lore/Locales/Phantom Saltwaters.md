A place for the restless souls - a colossal whirlpool inside which they've made a home, rife with their own exanimate economy, politics, and caste system. It is located north of [[Infernait]], and exists between The Fleeting Plane and The Sempiternal Plane.

Phantom Saltwaters have been formed over centuries atop centuries of passed sailors that have made their grave. The few shores it has are comprised of their bones and skulls, forever moving and spinning in the whirlpool.

The island in the centre of the whirlpool is where the Abysswater Parliament resides. The Abysswater Parliament is a group of six undead captains of different heritages, who alongside ruling the Phantom Saltwaters, command and control different regions & crews across the Fleeting, Sempiternal and Umbral planes.

Due to its rifeness with idolons and its proximity to the [[Serpentine Archipelago]], the [[Herald Morticia]] is known to pay this place a frequent visit.
