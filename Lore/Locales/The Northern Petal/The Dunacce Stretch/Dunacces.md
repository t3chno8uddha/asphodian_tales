Dunacces is a kingdom built almost entirely through the valley that stretches from the [[Freeflight Mountain Range]] to the field that leads to [[Brejur Academy]].

Its population is mostly comprised of farmers and craftsmen, and Dunaccans are known for their piousness.

They also make for excellent archers, saught after from many different fronts.

Due to the kingdom's geography, a lot of the old towns and districts are built directly inside mountanous caverns.

Although technically Galatean lands, due to Dunacces being an ocean away from the rest of the kingdom resulted in its diverse population and cultures.
