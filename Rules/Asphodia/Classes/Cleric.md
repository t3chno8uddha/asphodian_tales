- **For the Church** -{**P**}- +1♦ when pacifying, de-escalating, or addressing fellow clergy and the faithful.
- **Holy Conduit** -{**A**}-{**Arcane**}- Channel sacred energy to bless food, weapon, or ally; a blessed strike against the wicked exorcises them, and blessing purges malicious intent.
- **Healing Prayer** -{**S**}-{**Arcane**}- Cast a prayer that downgrades one of an ally's damages by a tier, or reattaches a lost limb.
- **Penance** -{**S**}-{**Physical**}- Take a wound or strain in an ally's place, granting them +1♦ as your sacrifice steadies them.
- **Miraculum** -{**S**}-{**Arcane**}- Cast a prayer that grants you or an ally Trivial Peril and Miraculous Payoff on their next action
