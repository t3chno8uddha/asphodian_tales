- **Third Eye** -{**A**}-{**Mental**}- Read the alignment and intent of any creature you see; +1♦ to perceive the hidden and unspoken, and by touch you may glimpse a mind's thoughts and memories.
- **Mantra** -{**S**}-{**Social**}- Utter a centering chant; you and each ally who hears it gain +1♦ on an Act of their own choosing for a short while. Strain yourself further to raise your boost to +2♦ for one Act, instead.
- **Unyielding Meditation** -{**S**}-{**Arcane**}- Channel in stillness to shed strain and purge your ailments; if struck while meditating, the blow is turned aside and your attacker falters.
- **Totemic Architect** -{**S**}-{**Arcane**}- Raise a totem that grants nearby allies +1♦ on physical or elemental Acts; seal a nearby spirit within it to lend its power, or give that spirit flesh to fight as a unit for a time.
- **Fate Weaver** -{**S**}-{**Arcane**}- Conduct a reading that bends fortune across a scene - aiding allies, hindering foes, stirring the world itself - its shape set by your interpretation and how firmly the threads answer you.
