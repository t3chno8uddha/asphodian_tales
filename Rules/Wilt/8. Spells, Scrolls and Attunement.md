Spells in the game are another kind of action that players can take. Unlike abilities, spells are also objects in the game's world.

Every spell names three things:
- **Act** - the single Act it demands. A spell cannot be performed any other way.
- **Cost** - the Strain the caster takes for using it.
- **Limit** - how many times a spell can be cast before depletion.

### Casting from a scroll:
A spell scroll is cast by rolling its Act, with Peril and Payoff as with any other action. The caster pays the spell's Cost upon attempt.
- **Failure** - *the spell does not happen. Its Limit is spent, and the Cost is paid.*
- **Partial success or better** - *the spell happens, and one use of its Limit is spent.*

An emptied scroll is not destroyed, only inert. Refilling one is a matter of narrative - a magician in a town of some standing, a shrine, a relic, a bargain - and never a matter of rest.

Scrolls take up space in a character's inventory. Attuned spells do not - they occupy their own box on the sheet.

### Attunement:
A character holding a full scroll may attempt to **Attune** it, binding the spell to themselves. Roll the Trait of the spell's Act, counting one die per non-zero Act in that Trait. Attuning inflicts strain the way a Resistance roll does.
- **Full success or critical** - *the spell is bound, and the scroll is consumed.*
- **Partial success** - *the binding fails. The scroll survives, and may be attempted again.*
- **Failure** - *the scroll is consumed, and the spell is lost with it.*

A character may hold at most ten attuned spells.
### Casting an attuned spell:
An attuned spell is cast by rolling its Act, with Peril and Payoff as with any other action. The caster pays the spell's Cost only if the spell lands.
- **Failure** - *the spell does not happen. Its Limit is not spent, and the Cost is not paid.*
- **Partial success or better** - *the spell happens, and one use of its Limit is spent.*

Attuned spells refill their Limit upon **Repose**.
